Frequently Asked Question

These are the most commonly asked questions.

What is UI?
It means User Interface. It is the look, feel, and interactive parts of a program or machine.
What is the difference between UI and UX?
UI is the visual design (colors, buttons, layout). UX is how easy and smooth the overall experience feels to the user.
What does a UI designer do?
They pick colors, fonts, and button sizes. They make the product look nice and easy to use.
